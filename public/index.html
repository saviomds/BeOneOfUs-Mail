How can we help?

Quick answers to help you get started.

How do I create an account?

Register with your @beoneofus.com company address. An administrator must approve your request before you can sign in.

Can I send real email?

This demo saves composed messages in your Sent folder. It does not deliver email.

Where is my profile saved?

Your profile and mailbox are saved to the app’s JSON database. File contents stay in this browser.
