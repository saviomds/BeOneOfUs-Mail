FIRST STEPS

Getting started

How do I create an account?

Register with your @beoneofus.com company email. An administrator must verify and approve your request before you can sign in or use a mailbox.

How do I update my profile?

Open your profile from the account area in your inbox. Choose “Edit profile” to change your display name, email address, or short introduction, then save your changes.

MAIL TOOLS

Using your inbox

Can I send and receive real email?

Not yet. BeOneOfUs Mail is currently a browser-based demo. Messages you compose are saved in your Sent folder, but they are not delivered to an email address.

How do drafts and attachments work?

Drafts and attachment details are saved in the app’s JSON database. You can attach files up to 5 MB each, with a 15 MB total per message. File contents remain in this browser and are not uploaded to a mail server.

What are the inbox folders for?

Use folders to review mail by status: Sent contains composed demo messages, Drafts contains saved drafts, and Archive, Spam, and Trash organize messages you move. Starred and labels help you find messages again.

YOUR DATA

Privacy & storage

Where is my profile and mail stored?

Account, profile, and mailbox data are stored in the app’s JSON database on the server. Attachment contents remain in this browser. The database file should be backed up and kept private.

Can I use my profile on another device?

You can sign in from another browser connected to the same server to access your profile and mailbox. Attachments are currently stored only in the browser where they were added.
